Request account deletion

Use this page to request permanent deletion of your HisabKitabx account and associated personal information.

This action is permanent. After your request is verified and processed, your deleted account cannot be restored.

How the process works

  1. Enter the email address registered with your HisabKitabx account.
  2. Select Send deletion request. Your email app will open with a prepared request.
  3. Send that email from your registered email address so we can verify ownership.
  4. We will process a valid request within 7 working days.

What happens to your data

✓ Data we delete

  • Your login account
  • Email, phone number and profile image
  • Device and notification tokens
  • Your Personal Expenses group and its data

i Data that may remain

  • Shared expenses and settlements needed for other members’ balances
  • Such records will be anonymized and shown as “Deleted User”
  • Records required by law, if applicable
